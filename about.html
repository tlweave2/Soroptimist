About Us

Soroptimist International City of Riverbank is one of the newest Soroptimist clubs in California. We were chartered with the help of our neighbors at Soroptimist International of Oakdale, and started out with 24 members. We meet twice a month to plan projects and fundraisers.

Most of us live or work in Riverbank. We're teachers, business owners, retirees, nurses and moms, and we joined because we wanted to do something useful for women and girls in our own town.

About Soroptimist

Soroptimist started in Oakland in 1921 and now has clubs in more than 120 countries. The name comes from Latin and roughly means "the best for women."

Soroptimist's mission is to improve the lives of women and girls through programs that lead to social and economic empowerment. In practice, that mostly means helping them get the education and training they need to support themselves.

Our club belongs to Soroptimist International of the Americas and the Sierra Pacific Region. You can read more at soroptimist.org.
