About Us

Best for women: a Riverbank club of volunteers working to improve the lives of women and girls.

Our mission

Improving the lives of women and girls

Soroptimist is a global volunteer organization that gives women and girls access to the education and training they need to achieve economic empowerment.

The name "Soroptimist" comes from the Latin soror, meaning sister, and optima, meaning best. Together it means "the best for women."

Our vision

Women and girls have the resources and opportunities to reach their full potential and live their dreams.

Our club

New to Riverbank

Soroptimist International City of Riverbank is the newest club in the Soroptimist Sierra Pacific Region. Our charter party was hosted by our sister club, Soroptimist International of Oakdale, and we started with 24 charter members.

We meet twice a month to plan our service projects and fundraisers.

Our roots

Part of a global sisterhood

  • Soroptimist was founded in 1921 in Oakland, California.
  • Clubs are active in more than 120 countries and territories.
  • Our club belongs to Soroptimist International of the Americas and the Sierra Pacific Region.
  • We partner with neighboring clubs, including Soroptimist International of Oakdale.
