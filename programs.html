Programs

Live Your Dream: Education and Training Awards for Women

The Live Your Dream Award is Soroptimist's main program. It gives cash to women who are the main financial support for their families and who are in school or a training program. The money isn't tied to tuition, so recipients can use it for childcare, rent, a car repair, or whatever is standing in the way.

Our club chooses local recipients, and they go on to compete for regional and international awards.

You may be eligible if you:

  • are the primary financial support for yourself and your dependents,
  • are enrolled in or accepted to a vocational program or an undergraduate degree, and
  • live in our area.

Applications open month and are due date. Apply online at soroptimist.org.

Dream It, Be It: Career Support for Girls

Dream It, Be It is a workshop for girls in middle and high school. They hear from women with all kinds of jobs, work through goal-setting exercises, and talk honestly about what gets in the way. A lot of the girls we hope to reach don't have many working women around them to learn from, and that's the gap this program fills.
