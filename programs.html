Our Programs

Education and training open doors. Our programs help women and girls in Riverbank walk through them.

Signature program

Live Your Dream: Education & Training Awards for Women

The Live Your Dream Awards give cash grants to women who are the primary financial support for their families. Recipients can use the award for anything that helps them reach their education goals: tuition, books, childcare, transportation and more.

Who can apply

  • Women who are the primary source of financial support for their families
  • Enrolled in, or accepted to, a vocational/skills training program or an undergraduate degree program
  • Living in our club's service area

For girls

Dream It, Be It: Career Support for Girls

Dream It, Be It connects girls in secondary school with professional women who serve as role models and mentors. Through interactive workshops, girls explore careers, set goals and learn to overcome obstacles.

  • Career exploration and goal-setting
  • Mentoring from local professional women
  • Practical skills for school and work
