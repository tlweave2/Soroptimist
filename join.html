Become a Member

Make friends, build skills and make a real difference for women and girls in Riverbank.

Why join

More than a club

  • Change lives through hands-on service in your own community
  • Build lasting friendships with like-minded women
  • Grow leadership, event-planning and public-speaking skills
  • Connect with a global network of Soroptimists
  • Be part of building a brand-new club from the ground up

How to join

  1. Reach out to us using the form on our contact page.
  2. Attend a meeting or mixer as our guest.
  3. Meet our members and see if it's a good fit.
  4. Complete a membership application.

Frequently asked questions

Who can join?

Soroptimist welcomes anyone who supports our mission of improving the lives of women and girls. Contact us and we'll share the details.

How much time does it take?

Our club meets twice a month, and members help with projects and fundraisers as their schedules allow.

Can I visit before deciding?

Absolutely. Guests are welcome at our meetings and mixers. Send us a note and we'll let you know when and where.

I can't commit to membership. Can I still help?

Yes! You can volunteer at events, mentor at Dream It, Be It, sponsor a fundraiser or make a donation.
