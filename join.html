Join

We're a new club, so this is a good time to get involved. You'd be helping decide what we work on and how we do it.

Members come to our two meetings a month when they can, help with a project or fundraiser here and there, and get to know other women in town. Nobody expects you to do everything.

How to join

  1. Send us a message or find us on Instagram.
  2. Come to a meeting or mixer as our guest.
  3. If it feels like a fit, fill out a membership application.

Annual dues are $ amount.

Questions

Who can join?
Anyone who supports our mission of helping women and girls. Get in touch and we'll go over the details.
How much time does it take?
It's up to you. We meet twice a month, and you can pitch in on projects as your schedule allows.
Can I come to a meeting first?
Yes, please do. Just let us know so we can save you a seat.
I can't commit right now. Can I still help?
Of course. Volunteer at an event, mentor at Dream It, Be It, or donate.
