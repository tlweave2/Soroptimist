Helping women and girls in Riverbank reach their goals

We're a group of Riverbank women who volunteer together. Through Soroptimist's programs, we help local women go back to school and help girls figure out what they want to do with their lives.

Join us   Read about our club

What we do

Live Your Dream Awards

Each year we give cash awards to local women who support their families and are working toward a degree or job training. They can spend it on whatever helps: tuition, books, childcare, gas.

Dream It, Be It

A career workshop for high school girls. They meet working women from our area, talk about jobs they might not have thought of, and leave with a plan.

Local projects

Smaller service projects and fundraisers here in town throughout the year.
