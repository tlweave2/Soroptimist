What we do

Our programs

Soroptimist programs focus on economic empowerment through education, so women and girls can build better lives for themselves and their families.

Live Your Dream Awards

Cash awards for women who are the primary financial support for their families and are going back to school or job training.

Learn more

Dream It, Be It

Career-support workshops that connect local girls with mentors and help them set goals and plan for the future.

Learn more

Community Service

Hands-on projects and fundraisers that support women, girls and families right here in Riverbank.

Learn more

Who we are

A new club rooted in Riverbank

Our club was chartered with the support of our neighbors at Soroptimist International of Oakdale, bringing Soroptimist's century-old mission home to the City of Riverbank.

Our members meet twice a month to plan service projects and fundraisers. We are friends, neighbors, professionals and parents who believe every woman and girl deserves the chance to live her dream.
